Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026.09.11
  Features:
    - Added a configuration API so other mods can retune the pipeline extent, underground distance, and pumping speed of any tier, including the vanilla iron tier this mod patches in place. See docs/modding-api.md. There are no new player-facing mod settings; this is a back-end surface for mod authors.
  Changes:
    - Moved prototype construction from the data stage to the data-updates stage, so a dependent mod's data.lua runs in between and can configure tiers before anything is built from them. Prototype names, values, and defaults are unchanged.
    - Extended the fluid-box reach this mod gives production machines to machines added by mods that load after it. The patch previously ran in the data stage and so only reached machines that already existed at that point.
    - Rejected configuration of a field a tier does not carry, such as an underground distance on the pumps-only high-pressure foundation tier, rather than accepting a change nothing would read.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026.08.24
  Compatibility:
    - Made Space Age an optional dependency, so the mod now loads on base Factorio 2.1. The ladder tops out at the reinforced tier without it, gated on production and utility science instead of the Space Age packs.
    - Stopped clobbering RampantFixed's non-freezing fluid line. Its pipes previously inherited this mod's upgrade chain, surface restrictions, and pipeline extent, so an upgrade planner could silently convert freeze-immune pipes into freezing ones. Their pipes and pipe-to-grounds now keep the iron-tier pipeline extent they have without this mod installed.
  Changes:
    - Renamed the heat-resistant tier to calcite-lined, for pipes, pipe-to-grounds, pumps, and offshore pumps. The tier never carried heat resistance; it is a steel component lined with calcite, unlocked by calcite processing, and its purpose is satisfying the Vulcanus surface condition. Existing saves migrate automatically.
    - Added a calcite-lined offshore pump, so lava can be extracted on Vulcanus before the tungsten tier. The Vulcanus branch previously had no offshore pump below tungsten.
    - Substituted refined concrete and low-density structure for tungsten plate and carbon fiber in the reinforced tier when Space Age is absent.
    - Gave each fluid role its own crafting menu row: pipes and pipe-to-grounds together, pumps, and offshore pumps, with planet-restricted variants in a secondary row beneath the progression they branch from.
  Bugfixes:
    - Made the foundation and high-pressure foundation tiers placeable on every surface. They inherited a pressure ceiling from the vanilla prototypes they copy, so the terminal tier covered fewer surfaces than the reinforced tier below it and could not be placed on Vulcanus at all.
    - Removed a declared cold resistance from the reinforced and foundation tiers. No cold damage type exists in Factorio, so the entry was silently discarded and the tiers never had the property their configuration claimed.
    - Corrected the reinforced pipe and pump technology descriptions, which described the tier as carbon-fiber reinforced. Without Space Age the recipes substitute refined concrete and low-density structure, and carbon fiber cannot be obtained at all, so the descriptions now name what the tier does rather than what it is made of.
